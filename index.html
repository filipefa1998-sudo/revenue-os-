Revenue Compliance Index™ (RCI) - The Moat

Unique 0-100 scoring system measuring sales discipline:

Category Weight What It Measures
Stage Discipline 30% % compliant transitions, gate violations, non-compliant days
Follow-Up Adherence 25% % on-time follow-ups, SLA adherence
Activity Velocity 20% Actual vs expected touch frequency per stage
Data Hygiene 15% Required fields, notes quality, duplicate rate
Forecast Accuracy 10% Forecasted vs actual variance (quarterly)

The RCI Formula:

RCI = (stage_score × 0.3) + 
      (followup_score × 0.25) + 
      (velocity_score × 0.2) + 
      (hygiene_score × 0.15) + 
      (forecast_score × 0.1)

Output: 0-100 (updated nightly)

Cooling Deal Radar™ - Risk Detection

🟢 GREEN

Trigger: All activities on track, within thresholds, positive engagement

Action: Continue current approach, celebrate compliance

🟡 YELLOW

Trigger: No activity X days, reply rate declining, missed follow-up

Action: AI suggests re-engagement, manager notified

🔴 RED

Trigger: No activity >2× threshold, stage duration >1.5× avg, sentiment declining

Action: Immediate escalation, manager intervention required

Revenue Compliance Index™ (RCI) - The Moat

Unique Scoring System: 0-100 score measuring sales discipline across 5 categories

Category Weight What It Measures Why It Matters
Stage Discipline 30% % of stage transitions compliant, gate violations, non-compliant days Prevents fake pipeline, ensures accurate forecasting
Follow-Up Adherence 25% % of required follow-ups completed on time, SLA adherence Prevents deal cooling, maintains momentum
Activity Velocity 20% Actual touch frequency vs expected per stage Measures engagement cadence, predictability
Data Hygiene 15% Required fields completion, notes quality, duplicate rate Ensures clean forecasting, reliable data
Forecast Accuracy 10% Forecasted vs actual variance (measured quarterly) Measures realism, ties discipline to outcomes

The RCI Formula:

RCI = (stage_score × 0.3) + 
      (followup_score × 0.25) + 
      (velocity_score × 0.2) + 
      (hygiene_score × 0.15) + 
      (forecast_score × 0.1)

Output: Single 0-100 index updated nightly. Managers see individual, team average, and 30-day trend.

Risk Level Trigger Conditions Action Required

• Within threshold ranges

Next-Best-Action Engine - AI-Powered Guidance

Priority Logic (Deterministic v1):

  1. Missing required activity → Suggest that specific activity
  2. Risk status (YELLOW/RED) → Suggest immediate call
  3. Stale deal (no touch in 3+ days) → Suggest follow-up email
  4. Stage progression ready → Suggest advancement activity

AI Drafting Layer: Uses OpenAI API + RAG to generate personalized messages based on:

  • Playbook content for current stage
  • Last 5 interactions with prospect
  • Company name and context
  • Stage-specific templates
